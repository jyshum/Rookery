# Sample images

Bundled lab photographs used as default annotation subjects. Chosen to look like
what a bench camera actually sees: wide frames with several distinct objects,
rather than close-up product shots.

Source: [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com), used under the

and non-commercial use without permission or attribution. Credited here anyway.
